About Us

We are a team of four final year undergraduates following the BSc (Hons) in Information Technology, specialising in Software Engineering, at SLIIT.

Who We Are

Research group R26-SE-018

Our project started from a simple observation. Orchid growers in Sri Lanka still look after their plants mostly by hand, and many decisions depend on years of experience. We wanted to see how far IoT devices and machine learning could support them in their daily work.

Each of us took one part of orchid care, researched it and built a working component. We then brought the four components together into one mobile app and tested it on a real orchid farm.
