Project Milestones

All the assessments of our research project, with their official dates and what was presented at each stage.

  1. 15 to 18 March 2026

    Project Proposal

    The proposal reports were submitted on 15 March and presented to the panel from 16 to 18 March. Each member presented the research problem, research gap, objectives and planned method of their component and how it fits into the complete system.

    CompletedNot marked separately
    Proposal reportsSlides
  2. 11 to 13 May 2026

    Progress Presentation 1

    The first progress review in front of the panel, covering the implementation of each component and the data collected up to that point. The checklist was submitted on 13 May.

    CompletedNot marked separately
    SlidesChecklist
  3. 31 August to 2 September 2026

    Progress Presentation 2

    The second progress review, with the components running inside the shared mobile app. For the smart watering component the panel asked how sensor placement is decided from data, how differences between sensors are handled, which evaluation metrics are used, how far one sensor node's reading can be trusted and how the watering times were checked.

    CompletedNot marked separately
    Slides

    Draft Thesis & Website Submission

    Submission of the draft thesis of each member and the complete source code of this website. The final checklist follows on 14 October.
